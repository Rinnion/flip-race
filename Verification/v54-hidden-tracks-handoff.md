## Final geometry and decisions

Both courses are 7920 units (36 seconds at base 220), corridor 240, same geometry for both profiles. Intercepts has three six-gate dense series, 18 two-part gates and three ordinary hidden 64×48 wall stones at exits. First two landing episodes also have air pairs; a later optional air reward follows each pad. Manifestation has four sections and 12 gates: sections two/four replace their first gate pair with an interior hidden stone, followed by the remaining visible passage and surface-change tasks. It has five hidden stones total. The sequences and surface states differ, rather than merely mirroring Intercepts.

The common module reuses the previously probed Cross/Low geometry. Local pad spacing was widened 100 units because Chosen otherwise reached the pad launch before finishing the previous low hop. No global optimizer or bot matrices were used. Safe routes skip all rewards. Compatible air-pair-plus-landing collection is allowed. Marked pads require actual support; Fast witnesses record real wall/stone landings and use the production air-repeat limit.
